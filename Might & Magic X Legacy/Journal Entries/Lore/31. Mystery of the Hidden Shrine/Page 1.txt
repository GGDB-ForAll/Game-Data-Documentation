Last night I awoke from the strangest of dreams,
A wandering soul appeared to me at Twilight's blurry
seams,
Telling of its long journey across a barren Void,
Seeking friendly ear for his stories to be told!


Summoned into battle, well-trained for war,
They left their underground cities for lands well afar,
Looking for the secrets hidden beyond their world's shell,
They must free a city from a succubus' vile spell.


They visit White Wolf and the Black Ridge,
Journey through marsh and hills, crossing many a bridge,
They defeat monsters and devils and omens of gloom,
And free a missing King from a fabled Doom.


Usurper! The heroes unseat you and restore freedom's song!
Gates are finally opened to the stars beyond.
The truth shall be revealed when seen through the lost Eye,
For our world was hollow, and now we have touched the sky!
