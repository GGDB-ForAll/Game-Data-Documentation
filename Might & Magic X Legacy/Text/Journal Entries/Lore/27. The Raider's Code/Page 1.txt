If Raider you wish to become or Raider you are, then this
book of code is paramount for working the trade without
sin.


	The First Rule - Life is more precious than gold
No man or woman shall be left behind, while breath
remains within them. Whether gold or jewel, relic or spell,
the value of a life is greater.


	The Second Rule - The price of life is life
If ever another Raider breaks the first rule and you make it
out alive to tell, then you are required to kill the scoundrel,
and make it clear that the only price for life is life.


	The Third Rule - A share is a share
Shares are equally divided between any Raider who survives
and risked life on the expedition whether treasure was
found in light or in shadow, with fight or none.
