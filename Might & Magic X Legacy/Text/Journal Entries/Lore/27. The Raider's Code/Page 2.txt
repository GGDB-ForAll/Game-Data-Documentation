	The Fourth Rule - Maps and Clues are paid for
Maps, clues and any information offered to the group are
bought and paid for with gold before the expedition. Only
those who go below the ground merit a share.


	The Fifth Rule - Pick first and pick well
Once a group has been formed, nothing shall break it until
all are returned safe to a town. Once the pact is made and
the expedition started, there is no turning back, no matter
what the cause.


	The Sixth Rule - Group before your Dragon
Worship your Dragon on your own time, and pray to
whichever you want. But when the raid has begun, the
group is your only religion. Protect your brothers and
sisters, and thank your Dragon after its done.


	The Seventh Rule - It's not stealing if the owner's dead
Raiders recover treasures from the dead and buried, not the
living. We recover those things forgotten and bring them
back to those who might use them for new purpose.
