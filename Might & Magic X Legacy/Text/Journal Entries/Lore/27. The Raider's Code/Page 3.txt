	The Eighth Rule - Treasure is treasure
No matter what the nature and power of the treasure you
might find, it is not for the Raider to judge whether it is
good or evil, nor a tool for right or wrong doing. Treasure is
Treasure. Relics do not kill: people do.


	The Ninth Rule - Never divide until it's safe
Never divvy up the loot until safely back in town. Sell it all
and count in gold. If you want something back, buy it from
the merchant who barters all the fare. Whether spell or
relic, map or tome, staff or sword, sell it all and divide the shares.


	The Tenth Rule - Rules are written to be bent
If ever a rule gets in the way of common sense and the
group can come to a mutual agreement, where everyone
agrees, then the new rule prevails.
