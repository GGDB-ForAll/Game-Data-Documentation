The Gods have spoken, a decree was signed,
The fates and prophets didn't seem to mind,
They'd put a conqueror on the path of war.
And so began their journey, a simple party of four.
A group of raiders to ordained Destiny blind,


The hordes marched on! Would our champions suffice?
To unite their clans against such wicked device.
Thwarting the vile progress of well planned invasion,
There are Gods without love for any mortal persuasion.
And challenging heaven comes not without price...
